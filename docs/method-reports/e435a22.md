# Report on the BMad Method at e435a22

This is the first report on the BMad Method (https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD).
It covers `main` at e435a22574fe55722841086e5566420178b98df3 of 2026-11-12.
That commit is an unreleased draft of v7.
The last release was v6.12.4 of 2026-10-14, from the maintenance branch V6.12.
That branch has `bmad-prd` and `bmad-create-epics-and-stories` instead.
The human chose the draft on 2026-11-19.
The draft's requirement ids, covers lines, rule for changed lines, and Set, Dependencies and Closure checks match plan items 4.2 to 4.4.
The next report compares the tag v7.0.0 with this commit.

## Taken

- Requirement lines `R<n>` that say what is possible and what shows it was met. They state WHAT, not HOW, and ids are never reused. Source: PO `references/spec.md`, "Spec Law".
  Why: a feature needs stable requirement ids so that tickets can cover them (plan items 4.2 and 4.3).
- The container sections Description, Outcome, Done when (three to six checks), Boundaries, Requirements and Constraints. Source: PO `assets/ticket-template.md` and PM `template-prd-light.md`.
  Why: a human can review a short document with these sections, and the template in `template.md` needs a fixed shape.
- Typed Decision, Assumption and Open question lines. Source: the Notes of `assets/ticket-template.md`.
  Why: stapel already has them by decision 0003, so the feature file uses the same lines.
- Elicitation, never authoring. An unaddressed domain implication becomes an open question. Source: PM `SKILL.md` and `spec.md`.
  Why: the intent belongs to the person, and the skill must ask instead of invent.
- The breakdown rules: one step toward Done when, split never shrink, tracer bullet first, tests in every ticket. Source: `slice.md`.
  Why: they give a repeatable way to cut a feature into tickets that fit one session.
- The Set and Dependencies checks: coverage of every requirement, real prerequisites, collisions, shared setup and handoffs. Source: `validate-checks.md`.
  Why: they become the checks `EL-<n>` in `checklist.md`, and plan item 4.4 needs the coverage they describe.

## Not taken

- Personas (the PM and PO agents).
  Why: the Frame non-goal says so; stapel has roles, not characters.
- Tracker stores and `tickets.toml`, the board, publishing.
  Why: plan item 4.1 excludes them, and the feature level lives in `.stapel`.
- `memlog.py`, `tickets.py` and any Python.
  Why: stapel is one binary with no second install (ledger 2026-11-10).
- The template engine (`{{ }}` and `{% %}`) and the `customize.toml` layers.
  Why: stapel has `stapel.toml`.
- Initiatives and epics as two container levels.
  Why: stapel has two levels only, feature and ticket (phase 4).
- The Closure check of a container against implementation evidence.
  Why: plan item 4.4 computes readiness from closed tickets and named tests.
- Given/When/Then story criteria.
  Why: stapel criteria use WHEN/THE and IF/THEN (decision 0001).
- Companions and adopted documents.
  Why: a feature is one file in this ticket.
- The refined flags, hitl, risk, plan and done checkpoints.
  Why: the route and the dialogs of stapel already cover them.
